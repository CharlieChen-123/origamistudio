Simulation & research

OrigamiSimulator by Amanda Ghassaei, with upstream acknowledgments to Sasaki Kosuke, Erik Demaine, and other contributors. Original copyright and MIT license are retained.

Research: Amanda Ghassaei, Erik Demaine, and Neil Gershenfeld, Fast, Interactive Origami Simulation using GPU Computation. Hypar reference: (Non)existence of Pleated Folds: How Paper Folds Between Creases.

Libraries & local references

Bundled dependencies include three.js, TrackballControls, SVGLoader, jQuery, Underscore, numeric.js, Earcut, FOLD, and path-data-polyfill. Their original notices are retained in the vendor files; see full third-party notices.

Local research references to Jun Mitani’s designs are credited to Jun Mitani. Those PDF and converted SVG artworks are excluded from this published library.

The project’s MIT license applies to project code; it does not establish ownership of third-party model designs or grant additional rights to artwork. Please retain attribution and consult original sources for reuse terms.
